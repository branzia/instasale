## App name (30 char max)
InstaSale

**Play Console listing title — use "InstaDM" instead.** Play's metadata
policy flags app names containing keywords that indicate price or
promotion, and "InstaSale" trips it on "Sale." This is a store-listing-
field workaround only — the in-app name, package id
(`com.branzia.InstaSale`), and all branding/docs stay "InstaSale."
"InstaDM" fits the same short, sales-focused, no-company-prefix naming
convention as the competitor apps ("Quick DM"/"Send DM") already cited
for the "InstaSale" choice itself. (7 chars — well under the 30 limit.)

## Full description (4000 char max)

InstaSale runs Branzia's Instagram Automation from your phone — comment
automation, a DM chatbot, Lead to Sale, and payments, all synced with
your Branzia web dashboard.

SIGN IN WITHOUT A PASSWORD
Open branzia.app/instagram on the web, tap "Connect Mobile App," and
scan the code with InstaSale. No email, no password — just a quick scan.

COMMENT & DM AUTOMATION
Auto-replies handle Instagram comments and DMs on your posts and reels
so no customer is left waiting. Build keyword-triggered rules, a DM
chatbot flow, or rich Card Messages with an image, headline, and
button — right from your phone.

POSTS & REELS
See your live Instagram media with automation coverage at a glance, and
set up (or edit) automation for any specific post — or your whole
account — in a few taps.

LEADS, CAPTURED AUTOMATICALLY
Conversations that show buying intent become Leads you can review and
follow up on, without digging through your DM inbox.

LEAD TO SALE
Send a payment link straight from a Lead. Once it's paid, it shows up
as a confirmed Order — track everything in one place.

PRODUCT CATALOG
Manage the products and custom attributes (like size or color, each
with its own price add-on) that power your DM chatbot's replies.

Requires an active Branzia Instagram Automation account, connected via
the Branzia web dashboard at branzia.app/instagram.
